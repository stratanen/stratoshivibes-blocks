Privacy Policy

Blockspace · Last updated: 27 October 2026 (game renamed to Blockspace)

Summary

Blockspace is a free puzzle game made by StratoshiVibes. It does not ask for an account and does not collect or sell personal data. Connecting a wallet is optional.

What is stored, and where

To let you resume a game and keep your best scores, the game saves a few small values on your own device using the browser's local storage:

  • the current game in progress (board, pieces, score)
  • your best score and how much of each picture you have revealed
  • your language and sound settings
  • your daily challenge game, today's best and your streak of days played
  • if you choose to connect a wallet: your public wallet address and which Vol.1 Degens characters it holds
  • if you buy an extra: what you bought, the transaction link, and your preferred currency

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Optional wallet connection

You can play without a wallet. If you choose to connect one, your wallet shares only your public address with the game. The game never asks your wallet to sign a message or a transaction, and it cannot move your funds.

To check which Vol.1 Degens cards you hold, the game sends your public address to Helius, a Solana data provider, which looks up public blockchain data. Your public address is already visible to anyone on the Solana blockchain. See Helius's privacy policy. Tap Disconnect at any time to remove the address from your device.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard is optional. To join, you sign one plain message with your wallet. It is not a transaction and cannot move funds; it only proves the address is yours. After that, the game sends your score at the end of each game to the StratoshiVibes leaderboard server, which runs on Cloudflare. The server stores your public wallet address, your best score per day (daily challenge), per week and overall, and when you set it. The leaderboard shows a shortened form of your address to everyone. Tap "Leave the leaderboard" to stop sending scores; to have your scores removed, contact us.

Purchases

When you buy an extra, the payment is a public Solana transaction from your wallet to the StratoshiVibes wallet, with a note naming the item (for example "svblocks:supporter"). Like every blockchain transaction, it is permanently visible to anyone. To show prices, the game asks Jupiter for current SOL and SKR prices; this request does not include your address. "Restore purchases" reads your wallet's public transaction history through Helius.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up or email collection (joining the leaderboard uses your wallet, not an account)
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools
  • No signing requests, except the single transfer you approve when you buy an extra in the Shop
  • No tokens or real-money rewards. The only purchases are the optional Shop extras described above.

Hosting

The game files are served from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version handles data in a new way, this page will be updated before that version is released.
