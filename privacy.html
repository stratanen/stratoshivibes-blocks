Privacy Policy

StratoshiVibes Blocks · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary

StratoshiVibes Blocks is a free puzzle game. It does not ask for an account, does not connect to your wallet, and does not collect or sell personal data.

What is stored, and where

To let you resume a game and keep your best scores, the game saves a few small values on your own device using the browser's local storage:

  • the current game in progress (board, pieces, score)
  • your best score and how much of each picture you have revealed
  • your language setting

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up, login, or email collection
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools
  • No wallet connection, transactions, or signing requests
  • No in-app purchases, tokens, or real-money rewards

Hosting

The game files are served from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data, such as an optional wallet connection, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/stratanen/stratoshivibes-blocks.
